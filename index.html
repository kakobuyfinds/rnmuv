Before you order with iTaoBuy

Confirm the source link

Open the CAFANS product page and locate the buying link. Check that the agent accepts the seller URL and that the listing still offers the colour, size and quantity you selected.

Record measurements for QC

Save the relevant measurements in your shortlist. For a tee, compare chest width and garment length with one you own. If warehouse photos are available, check the variant, measurements and visible defects before approving shipment.

Compare the delivered total

Keep item price, domestic delivery, agent fees, international shipping and applicable import charges in separate columns. Compare quotes for the same destination and packed dimensions, and check which costs each quote includes.

Frequently asked questions

What is an iTaoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product research list you can use before placing an order through iTaoBuy. This one brings together CAFANS product links, estimated item prices and space for your own size and QC notes.

How do I open my shortlist in Google Sheets?

Select the products you want, add any notes, and download the CSV. In Google Sheets, choose File → Import and upload the file. The download includes the CAFANS listing URL for each selected item.

Does the USD estimate include shipping?

The estimate covers the item price shown by CAFANS. Confirm the current price for your variant, shipping and applicable fees before ordering.

Where can I find quality-control photos?

If your agent provides warehouse QC photos, look for them in the corresponding order after the item arrives. Match the photos to the exact variant and request any missing measurements before dispatch.
